Statement

I work in two temperatures. One is available light — a bride under tungsten, an executive at a tall window, a rapper leaning into the booth at two in the morning. You get one pass at it and the room decides most of the exposure.

The other is engineered — vector marks, jersey panels, tournament key art built to survive at forty pixels on a phone. Nothing is found; every edge is a decision.

Same eye. Different physics. The work below is sorted that way on purpose.
